# Carter's, Inc.

> Carter's, Inc. (NYSE: CRI) is the largest branded marketer of young children's apparel in the
> United States, operating the Carter's, OshKosh B'gosh and Skip Hop brands through its own retail
> stores, its e-commerce sites, and wholesale partners. Carter's publishes **no public developer
> API, developer portal, or partner API program**. Its B2B integration surface is EDI (X12) trading
> partnership brokered through third-party VANs and EDI vendors, not a first-party API. This file is
> generated by API Evangelist from the catalog record; it is not published by Carter's.

## Status

- No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, WSDL, Protobuf or Postman collection is published anywhere on
  a Carter's-controlled host (probed 2026-09-05).
- No MCP server, no A2A agent card, no /.well-known/ discovery document on any of eight hosts.
- No first-party SDK on npm, PyPI, RubyGems, Maven Central, NuGet, crates.io or pkg.go.dev.
- The consumer-commerce hosts (carters.com, www.carters.com, api.carters.com, www.oshkosh.com,
  www.skiphop.com) sit behind a Cloudflare managed challenge and answer HTTP 403 to non-browser
  clients, so they are not machine-readable at all.
